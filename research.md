My research and applied work focus on making marketing and business decisions more rigorous, useful, and scalable.

## Causal Measurement & Experimentation
Marketing Mix Modeling, incrementality, causal inference, experimentation, and budget optimization. See [DeepCausalMMM](/deepcausalmmm/) and [selected publications](/publications/).

## Marketing Measurement & Decision Intelligence
Attribution, response curves, budget allocation, and decision support for marketing investment. This work turns analytical methods into practical systems for planning, measurement, and action.

See the [conference research](/publications/#conference-research) and [talks](/talks-service/) for selected public work in this area.

## AI & Decision Systems
LLM-assisted analytics, agentic AI for measurement, and decision delegation in marketing measurement. See [The AI Delegation Blind Spot](/publications/#working-papers) and the [accepted AMA conference paper](/publications/#conference-research).

## Predictive Brand, Product & Marketing Data Science
Predictive modeling, synthetic-data evaluation, and rare-event prediction for brand, product, and marketing applications.
